the rule

Every PR brings a Lean contract.
You read the Lean.

The contract is the story of the change: what it may do, and what it must never do. CI checks that the contract is there and that Lean accepts it — not the generated code. A person reads the Lean. That is the review.

What happens on a PR

1 · bring a contract

Check in a Lean file under plugin/retellings. Say what the change may do and what it must never do.

2a · still matches

The contract still matches the old rules. The change did not move the line.

2b · the contract changed

The contract itself moved. That is fine only if you meant to. The new contract is what you review.

What CI checks

That a contract file is on the PR. That Lean accepts it. That nothing is unfinished. That the contract does not prove a contradiction.

CI does not read the product code for you. You do, by reading the Lean.
